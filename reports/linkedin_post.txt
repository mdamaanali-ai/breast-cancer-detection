🚀 Built a Breast Cancer Detection ML Project | Neural Networks + Responsible Evaluation

For my internship project, I built a complete breast-cancer classification workflow using the supplied Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer dataset.

🔍 What I implemented:
• Data cleaning and leakage-safe preprocessing
• Malignant vs. benign classification
• A neural network with BatchNorm + Dropout + AdamW
• Benchmarking against Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost
• Confusion matrix, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, sensitivity/recall and specificity
• Validation-based threshold selection to prioritize malignant-case sensitivity
• Permutation-based feature importance
• Reusable inference workflow
• A short visual demo video

📊 Test-set headline: the final neural network achieved approximately 97% accuracy with high sensitivity on the held-out test set.

⚠️ This is an educational ML project, not a medical diagnostic tool. Real clinical deployment requires prospective validation, calibration, clinical oversight, regulatory review and external datasets.

I’m especially proud of the evaluation approach: in a cancer-screening context, accuracy alone is not enough. False negatives matter.
